Miao Village is a 2D animation project inspired by the Miao culture of Guizhou, China. It follows a village girl and a mythical dragon, from their childhood encounter to a reunion years later. Mountain villages, traditional clothing, and festival imagery shape a fantastical world, using watercolor textures, bold colors, and soft lighting to explore growing up, memory, and belonging.

## My Role

I independently developed the project, from story concept and character and environment development to AI-assisted image creation, animation production, and editing. The title sequence introduces the project’s visual world and atmosphere.

**Tools：** Midjourney · Nano Banana · Kling
