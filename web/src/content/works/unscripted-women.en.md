## About the Project

Created for the Ways of Knowing course, this magazine examines how gender, colonial histories, religion, and class shape women’s lives through three films: Wadjda, Black Girl, and The Handmaiden. Combining film analysis with editorial design, it explores how women negotiate constraints and assert agency across different cultural contexts.
