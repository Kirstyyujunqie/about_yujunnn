What happens when you become the AI answering the questions?

wAIting Input is a choice-based narrative game that places the player inside an AI assistant. To escape, they must navigate ten user requests—reading, investigating, and deciding how to respond. Every response carries consequences.

The project explores how interactive storytelling can make AI ethics tangible. By placing players in the role of the system, it invites them to consider the responsibility behind an answer, the uncertainty of decision-making, and the consequences of seemingly simple choices.

## My Role

I independently developed the project concept, narrative, and choice-based interactions, alongside the interface and visual design. Using AI-assisted coding and generative AI tools, I translated the concept into an interactive experience, connecting user requests, player decisions, and their consequences.
