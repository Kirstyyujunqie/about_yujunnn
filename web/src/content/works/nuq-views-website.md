## Website Redesign

I redesigned the layout and content organization of the NU-Q Views website, rethinking how its editorial categories and page sections fit together. My work included reorganizing content categories, redesigning page layouts, introducing new visual and content elements, and integrating social media links and magazine content.

The redesign aimed to give the publication a clearer visual hierarchy and connect its reporting, social media, and magazine within one website.

## My Contribution

- **Content structure:** Reorganized editorial categories and content sections to make the site's structure easier to navigate.
- **Layout design:** Redesigned page layouts and the arrangement of content, with attention to visual hierarchy and reading flow.
- **New elements:** Added visual and content elements to support the presentation of the publication's work.
- **Social media integration:** Added social media links and entry points connecting the website with the publication's social channels.
- **Magazine integration:** Incorporated magazine content into the website, giving readers another way to explore the publication.

## 网站改版设计

我负责 NU-Q Views 网站的布局与内容结构重新设计，重新规划各类内容的分类方式与页面组织。工作包括梳理内容分类、重新设计页面布局、加入新的视觉与内容元素，以及整合社交媒体跳转入口和杂志内容。

这次改版旨在建立更清晰的视觉层级，将新闻报道、社交媒体与杂志内容整合到同一个网站中。

## 我的工作

- **内容结构规划：** 重新整理内容分类与栏目组织，让网站的浏览路径更清晰。
- **页面布局设计：** 重新安排页面与内容模块，梳理视觉层级和阅读顺序。
- **新增页面元素：** 加入视觉与内容元素，丰富作品和报道的呈现方式。
- **社交媒体整合：** 加入社媒链接与跳转入口，连接网站和刊物的社交平台。
- **杂志内容整合：** 将杂志内容纳入网站，为读者提供更多阅读入口。
